RF SENSING MECHANISM & PHYSICAL PRINCIPLES

1. 5 GHz Multipath Scattering

At 5.24 GHz, electromagnetic waves penetrate drywall and non-metallic obstacles. The human body is ~70% water with high dielectric permittivity, strongly scattering and reflecting the signal into complex constructive and destructive interference waves.

2. Microsecond Dispersion (MAC Jitter)

Human motion inside the Fresnel zone disrupts phase coherence, causing micro-drops in SNR. 802.11ax hardware dynamically triggers PHY retransmissions and MCS adaptation, producing microsecond-scale RTT latency bursts measured in real-time.

3. Respiration vs Motion Bandpass

Normal breathing displaces the human chest wall by ~0.5–1.2 cm at 0.15–0.45 Hz (9–27 breaths/min). Human walking and movement generate Doppler perturbations in the 0.5–3.0 Hz band, extracted cleanly using real-time FFT spectral decomposition.
